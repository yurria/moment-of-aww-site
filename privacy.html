Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 5, 2026

Moment of Aww is a small shop that makes custom acrylic shoe charms. This policy covers the internal order-processing tool we use to run the shop (the Google OAuth app "moment-of-aww-print"). It is used only by the shop owner; it is not offered to the public.

What Google access the tool uses

The tool requests one Google permission: https://www.googleapis.com/auth/drive.file. This lets it see and manage only the files and folders it creates itself in the shop's Google Drive (for example preview images, print order files and an approval spreadsheet). It cannot see any other file in the Drive, and it does not read Gmail, contacts, calendar or other Google data.

What data we handle

Order photos and order details (such as names, sizes and shipping addresses) come from the shop's Etsy orders. We use them only to make and ship the ordered charms.

Sharing

We do not sell or share this data with third parties, except what is needed to fulfil the order: the printing partner receives the print files, and the carrier receives the shipping details.

Retention and deletion

Order files are kept for one year in case of reorders, then deleted. Preview and print files uploaded to Google Drive are moved to the trash after 30 days. To ask for your data to be deleted sooner, email momentofaww@gmail.com.
